## Status vocabulary

`Saved` → `Applied` → `Screen` → `Interviewing` → `Offer` → `Accepted`
Terminal alternatives: `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`, `Ghosted` (no reply 21+ days).

## Follow-up rules

- Applied with no update in 7 days: suggest a polite check-in.
- Interviewing with no update in 7 days: suggest a thank-you or status note.
- Offer: surface the response deadline in every pipeline review.
