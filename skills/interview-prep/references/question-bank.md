# Question bank by category

Select and adapt; every role-specific question should cite a job description
line.

## Role-specific (derive from JD)

- "Walk me through how you have done [core responsibility] before."
- "The role needs [required skill]. Describe the most complex situation where
  you used it."
- "How would you approach [first-90-days problem implied by the JD]?"

## Behavioral (STAR)

- A time you disagreed with a decision and what you did.
- A project that failed or slipped; what you changed afterwards.
- Influencing without authority.
- Handling an ambiguous or under-specified request.
- Delivering under a hard deadline.
- Receiving tough feedback.

## Situational / case

- "If [metric from JD] dropped 20% next quarter, how would you investigate?"
- "You inherit [system/process named in JD] with no documentation. First
  week?"
- Prioritization: two urgent stakeholders, one afternoon.

## Motivation and logistics

- Why this role and company (answer must use JD or user-supplied facts).
- Why leaving the current role (neutral, forward-looking).
- Compensation expectations (route to `offer-negotiation` guidance: give a
  range the user has decided on, or defer politely).
- Start date, location, work authorization as applicable.

## Questions to ask the interviewer (make them JD-specific)

- "The posting mentions [X]. What does success on that look like at six
  months?"
- "What is the biggest obstacle the team hit on [responsibility] this year?"
- "How does this role interact with [team named in JD]?"
- "What would make you say the hire was a great one a year from now?"

## STAR answer format

- **Situation**: one sentence of context.
- **Task**: what the user was accountable for.
- **Action**: 2–3 specific steps the user personally took.
- **Result**: outcome with a real number, or `[add metric]`.
