# JobCraft

A plugin for AI chat assistants that turns your resume and a job description
into a structured job-search workflow. Everything runs on text you share;
nothing is fetched from job boards or sent on your behalf.

## What it does

- **Tailor your resume** to a job description with a keyword-gap table,
  rewritten bullets grounded in your real experience, and ATS format checks.
- **Draft a cover letter** that flags any claim it cannot verify from your
  materials.
- **Prepare for interviews** with likely questions, STAR answer drafts built
  from your resume, and questions to ask the interviewer.
- **Plan an offer negotiation** with a priority matrix, counter-offer script,
  and email template using the numbers you provide.
- **Track applications** in a simple table you own and update in chat.

## What it does not do

- Search or scrape job boards or LinkedIn.
- Submit applications, send emails, or message recruiters.
- Provide live salary or market data.
- Invent experience, titles, dates, or metrics.

## Get started

Install JobCraft from your assistant's plugin directory, then paste your
resume and a job description and say what you need, for example
"Tailor my resume for this job description."
