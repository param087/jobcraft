# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

Career Copilot ("the plugin") is a skills-only plugin for ChatGPT and Codex
published by Param Bhavsar ("we").

## What the plugin is

The plugin consists solely of instruction files (skills) that ChatGPT loads
to guide its responses. It has no server, database, API, or network
component operated by us.

## Data we collect

**None.** The plugin does not collect, transmit, store, or have access to any
personal data. Resumes, job descriptions, offer details, and application
records you share stay within your ChatGPT conversation and are processed by
OpenAI under OpenAI's privacy policy and terms, not by us.

## Data categories, purposes, recipients, retention

Because we receive no data, there are no categories of personal data
collected by us, no purposes of processing by us, no recipients, and no
retention period. We have no ability to access, export, or delete your
conversation data; use ChatGPT's own controls for that.

## Third parties

The plugin does not call third-party services, does not browse the web, and
does not integrate with job boards or other platforms.

## Children

The plugin is intended for general audiences aged 13 and over and does not
knowingly target children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date above reflects the latest revision.
